\title{Radial Module 4 equation in general dimension}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

Take the same real scalar field and potential as Module 4,
\[
  V(\phi)=\frac{1}{2}\phi^2-\frac{1}{4}\phi^4,
  \qquad V'(\phi)=\phi-\phi^3.
\]
The damped field equation is
\[
  \partial_t^2\phi-\nabla_d^2\phi+V'(\phi)
  +\gamma(r)\partial_t\phi=0.
\]
For a spherically symmetric field in dimension \(d\),
\[
  \nabla_d^2\phi
  =\frac{1}{r^{d-1}}\partial_r\!\left(r^{d-1}\partial_r\phi\right)
  =\partial_r^2\phi+\frac{d-1}{r}\partial_r\phi.
\]
Thus the equation used by the dimension sweep is
\[
  \boxed{\partial_t^2\phi
  =\partial_r^2\phi+\frac{d-1}{r}\partial_r\phi
  -\phi+\phi^3-\gamma(r)\partial_t\phi.}
\]
Regularity requires \(\partial_r\phi(0,t)=0\), with
\(\nabla_d^2\phi(0,t)=d\,\partial_r^2\phi(0,t)\). The calculation uses
\(\phi(r,0)=A_0\exp[-r^2/(2R_0^2)]\), \(\partial_t\phi(r,0)=0\), and
\(\phi(R_{\max},t)=0\). Real \(d>0\) is interpreted as a continuous
parameter in the radial operator (analytic continuation), not as an ordinary
integer-dimensional spatial grid.
